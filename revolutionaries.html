“Revolutionary” is used here as a broad historical descriptor for people linked to underground, militant, armed or overseas anti-colonial activity. It is not a moral ranking. Many other leaders, organisers and ordinary participants advanced independence through constitutional work, mass mobilisation, labour, peasant, social reform and non-violent action.
